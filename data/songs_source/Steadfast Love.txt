Steadfast Love

You have called me by name
You have written my story in grace
Steadfast love that never runs dry
Steadfast love, I will seek Your face

Your mercy meets me every morning
Your kindness follows all my days
I will sing of Your steadfast love
I will lift my voice in praise

Nothing can separate me from You
Nothing can silence this song I sing
Steadfast love, forever steady
Steadfast love, my everything
